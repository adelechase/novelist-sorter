NoveList Results Sorter

The below bookmarklet collects an entire NoveList search, 50 results per page, into a sortable table with titles, authors, Goodreads ratings, publication dates, linked Goodreads review counts, synopses, and subjects.

Add the bookmarklet

  1. Show the bookmarks toolbar (in Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag the purple button below onto it. Do not click it on this instruction page.
  2. Open NoveList at the library, run your search, and go to page 1. Close any sorter window that is already open.
  3. Click the new bookmark, then Collect all pages (50 per page). It automatically selects Show: 50, waits for the results, expands the subjects, and collects every page.

Sorting

Click Title, Author, Goodreads rating, Published (In:), Goodreads reviews, or Series to sort. Click a heading again to reverse its direction. Each clicked heading becomes the primary sort, while the previous sort levels remain as tie-breakers. Arrows show direction and numbers show priority.

Example: To group by author and show each author’s highest-rated books first, click Goodreads rating first, then Author. The priorities become 1. Author ascending, 2. Goodreads rating descending. Click Author again to reverse author order while keeping the rating order within each author.

For several levels, click the lowest-priority column first and the highest-priority column last. Clear sort returns to collection order. Missing values sort last in either direction. Synopsis and Subjects are not sortable.

Collected information

The synopsis includes the full text present in the results card, even when NoveList visually truncates it. Collection expands “+ more” subjects lists before reading them. If a subject list cannot expand, its row is marked and the completion message reports it.

The review count retains NoveList’s text and Goodreads link, and sorts numerically. Each book cover appears below its title in the same cell. The Title column sorts by title text only. Titles open their original NoveList records in new tabs. Export CSV includes all fields and both book links in the current sort order.

Collect and import series information

  1. Collect your NoveList search with the sorter. Each Series cell includes a Goodreads link and a link to Fantastic Fiction’s home page, where you can search for the book.
  2. Open an individual Goodreads or Fantastic Fiction book page. Click Series Helper, review the detected title, author, series name, and book number, then click Save this book. You may correct any field. Choose No only when you have confirmed the book is a standalone.
  3. Repeat for as many books as you want to check. Each website keeps its own saved records. The helper reads only the currently open book page.
  4. Click Export collected series on each website. This downloads goodreads-series.json or fantasticfiction-series.json.
  5. Return to the sorter and click Import series. Select one or both files; multiple files are supported. You can repeat imports as you check more books.

Goodreads book numbers come from the series heading above the title when present. Fantastic Fiction book numbers come from its book-page metadata, such as “The third book in the … series.” Known positions with an unknown total display as Book 3 of ?. If a total is explicitly available or you enter a verified total, the cell can show Book 3 of 5.

Yes means a series is identified but its number is unknown, or the sources disagree about the book number. No requires standalone evidence saved from both sources. Unknown means no matching import or insufficient evidence. A series marked on either source takes precedence over a standalone indication on the other.

Records match by Goodreads book ID, or by title and author together. Author order such as “Tingle, Chuck” versus “Chuck Tingle” is handled. Unmatched records are reported; you can edit the helper’s title/author to match the NoveList entry exactly before exporting again. Hover over a Series cell for source notes. CSV export includes the series label and notes.

Saved helper records remain in each website’s browser storage until you click Clear saved records. Imported information remains in the current sorter window; reimport after closing or reloading it. No background tabs or cross-site requests are made by the helper.
