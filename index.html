NoveList Results Sorter

The below bookmarklet collects an entire NoveList search, 50 results per page, into a sortable table with titles, authors, Goodreads ratings, publication dates, linked Goodreads review counts, synopses, and subjects.

Add the bookmarklet

  1. Show the bookmarks toolbar (in Firefox: Ctrl+Shift+B), then drag the purple button below onto it. Do not click it on this instruction page.
  2. Open NoveList at the library, run your search, and go to page 1. Close any sorter window that is already open.
  3. Click the new bookmark, then Collect all pages (50 per page). It automatically selects Show: 50, waits for the results, expands the subjects, and collects every page.

Sorting

Click Title, Author, Goodreads rating, Published (In:), or Goodreads reviews to sort. Click a heading again to reverse its direction. Each clicked heading becomes the primary sort, while the previous sort levels remain as tie-breakers. Arrows show direction and numbers show priority.

Example: To group by author and show each author’s highest-rated books first, click Goodreads rating first, then Author. The priorities become 1. Author ascending, 2. Goodreads rating descending. Click Author again to reverse author order while keeping the rating order within each author.

For several levels, click the lowest-priority column first and the highest-priority column last. Clear sort returns to collection order. Missing values sort last in either direction. Synopsis and Subjects are not sortable.

Collected information

The synopsis includes the full text present in the results card, even when NoveList visually truncates it. Collection expands “+ more” subjects lists before reading them. If a subject list cannot expand, its row is marked and the completion message reports it.

The review count retains NoveList’s text and Goodreads link, and sorts numerically. Each book cover appears below its title in the same cell. The Title column sorts by title text only. Titles open their original NoveList records in new tabs. Export CSV includes all fields and both book links in the current sort order.

Collection

Keep the tab open without changing the search or filters. Collection uses up to 50 results on each page, including the shorter final page. Stop retains already collected rows; Close discards the table. Add current page expands and collects just that page without switching the page size.

Progress shows how many results were collected and flags incomplete collections. Selecting 50 changes NoveList’s own results-per-page preference.

Ratings are read from the precise star-fill widths. Publication sorting uses the displayed month and year; dates showing only a year use January as the sorting key.
